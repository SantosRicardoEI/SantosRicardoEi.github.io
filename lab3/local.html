Location

Tokyo is located on the eastern coast of Honshu Island, in the Kantō region. It borders Tokyo Bay on the Pacific Ocean and is Japan's political, economic and cultural centre.

Where is it?

The city stretches across a broad plain and is made up of 23 special wards, each with its own character. On clear days, Mount Fuji can be seen to the west.
