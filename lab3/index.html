Tokyo is the capital of Japan and one of the world's most populous and dynamic cities. Here, centuries-old temples stand alongside skyscrapers, neon lights and cutting-edge technology.

Discover Tokyo

Its best-known districts include Shibuya, famous for its busy crossing; Asakusa, home to Sensō-ji Temple; and Akihabara, the centre of technology and video-game culture.

  • Traditional temples, shrines and gardens
  • Japanese cuisine for every taste
  • A fast and efficient transport network
