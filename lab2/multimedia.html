Poetry

Tokyo shines beneath the rain,

Neon lights and midnight trains.

Crowded streets, a restless glow,

A city always on the go.


Ancient temples, quiet skies,

Cherry blossoms softly rise.

Old and new beneath one light,

Tokyo dreams into the night.
